# Roadmap

## Current baseline

Opptium has a persisted Company Profile and immutable Campaign Strategy model, durable provider-backed research, campaign-local lead review and Outreach, verified-contact provenance, grounded drafts, usage history, frozen exports, authorized downloads, live run progress, tenant integration tests, and browser workflow coverage.

## Completed refactor baseline

Company Profile analysis, schema-validated strategy generation, Tavily-backed approved-company enrichment, route verification provenance, durable grounded batch draft generation, immutable usage events, workspace-scoped export history, authorized historical CSV downloads, and authenticated durable-run progress surfaces are implemented. Disposable-database tenant isolation and persisted campaign-workflow integration coverage run in CI. The Playwright journey covers authentication, onboarding, Company Profile versioning, campaign and Strategy creation, synthetic completed enrichment, recipient acceptance, draft editing, export recording, and historical download.

## Future product work

Automatic email sending, mailbox creation, follow-ups, reply detection, CRM integrations, advanced billing, and agency features remain outside the locked MVP.
